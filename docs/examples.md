# Examples

Three notebooks read small pieces of public stores: two on AWS Open Data and
one image on the Image Data Resource. They are shown here with the outputs
saved in the notebooks, and each opens
in Google Colab.

```{toctree}
:maxdepth: 1

examples/mur_sst
examples/cmip6_tas
examples/ome_zarr_idr0051
```

| Notebook | Colab | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sea surface temperature | [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/leehart3000/zarrextract/blob/main/notebooks/mur_sst.ipynb) | MUR SST from NASA JPL PO.DAAC, via the [Registry of Open Data on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/mur). No restrictions on use. |
| Climate model air temperature | [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/leehart3000/zarrextract/blob/main/notebooks/cmip6_tas.ipynb) | CMIP6 MRI-ESM2-0 historical `tas`, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [doi:10.22033/ESGF/CMIP6.6842](https://doi.org/10.22033/ESGF/CMIP6.6842). |
| Light-sheet microscopy (OME-Zarr) | [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/github/leehart3000/zarrextract/blob/main/notebooks/ome_zarr_idr0051.ipynb) | IDR idr0051, Steventon et al., [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), [doi:10.17867/10000117](https://doi.org/10.17867/10000117).
